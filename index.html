Thesis spotlight

03

A Deep Learning Framework for High-Fidelity MRI Image Reconstruction

A self-supervised reconstruction pipeline built for undersampled MRI, evaluated on the NYU fastMRI multi-coil brain dataset.

  • Combined a GSURE denoiser, an EDM score network (DDPM++ backbone, 65.5M parameters), and DPS-based inference.
  • Trained and evaluated on 3T Siemens multicoil brain scans (AXT2 sequence, 20 receive coils): 295 training, 267 validation, 10 evaluation slices.
  • Reconstructed scans undersampled at 4x acceleration (R=4).
